A closer look at the coat

Grooming means looking after the coat, mane, and tail. Brushes help lift loose dirt and hair. While grooming, a handler has a chance to notice changes such as a scrape or a tender spot.

Learn where to stand

Ask an experienced adult which brush to use and where it’s safe to stand. If the horse moves away or a spot seems sore, pause and tell the adult so they can decide whether a veterinarian should take a look.
