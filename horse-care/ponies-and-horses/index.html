Size tells you how tall an animal is. Getting to know the individual tells you much more about how it might act.

Get to know the individual

One pony might explore a new object right away. Another might hang back, just as a larger horse might. Researchers have found some differences between breeds, and they also find that personality varies from one animal to another. Experience and how an animal is kept can matter too.

Watch with someone who knows them

Ask an experienced adult to show you what relaxed or worried behavior looks like in a horse or pony they know. Before approaching, ask the handler where to stand and whether the animal is comfortable being greeted.
