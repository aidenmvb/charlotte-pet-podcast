A rinse or a bath?

Washing usually means rinsing away dirt or sweat with water. An experienced handler can decide whether a small area needs rinsing or whether a full bath makes sense for that horse and weather.

Set up with an adult

An experienced adult checks the footing, water temperature, and water pressure and makes sure the horse is comfortable. Ask where you can help. If you spot a sore or irritated patch of skin, tell the adult so they can ask a veterinarian about it.
