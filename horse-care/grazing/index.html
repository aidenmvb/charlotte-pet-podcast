Eating a little at a time

Grazing means eating grass and other suitable plants as a horse moves through a pasture. Grass and hay are examples of forage, the fibrous plants that make up much of a horse’s diet.

Every horse has its own plan

A horse’s needs depend on its health, activity, and what’s growing in the field. Ask the adult in charge before opening a gate or offering extra grass. A veterinarian can help plan pasture access for a horse with a health condition.
