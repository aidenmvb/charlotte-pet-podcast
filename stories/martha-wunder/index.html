Checking on Crispy

Charlotte’s visit to check on Crispy is a starting point for thinking about horse care. Checking in on a familiar horse and helping a horse get comfortable with something new are two different moments that make Charlotte curious about what a trainer pays attention to. Horse trainer Martha Wunder is someone she wants to ask.

What Charlotte wonders

  • How can a trainer tell when a horse needs more time to get comfortable with something new?
  • What might you notice when you check in on a horse you know well?
  • How do you decide when to try again and when to give the horse a break?
