What this story is

This story stays at home. It follows three very young puppies as they grow, play, and find out what is around them. Later photos and video can show the same three as they change, in the order they were taken, earliest first.

Their names are not on the site yet. The page will use the names that come with the photos, rather than stand-in names.

What the story is following

  1. What are the three puppies doing while they are still tiny?

  3. What does taking care of them at home look like from one day to the next?
