What this story is

This one is a visit, not a sit-down interview. Charlotte went to see a baby elephant at the National Zoo, and to look at the work of the people who care for the animals there. The photos from that visit will go on this page.

The elephant's name is not on the site yet. It will be added with the photos if Charlotte has it. These notes do not describe the visit as if it had already been written up.
