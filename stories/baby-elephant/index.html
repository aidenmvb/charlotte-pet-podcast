A visit to the zoo

Charlotte went to the National Zoo to see a baby elephant. Seeing a young elephant as a visitor made her curious about the parts of its day she couldn’t see. She’d like to know what keepers pay attention to as the elephant grows and what their work looks like on an ordinary day.

What Charlotte wonders

  • What does a typical day caring for a baby elephant look like?
  • What changes do keepers watch for as a young elephant grows?
  • How do keepers get to know what an elephant needs?
